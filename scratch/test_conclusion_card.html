✨ Governance & Executive Summary

The completed analysis demonstrates that aggregate emergency department demand should not be interpreted through visit volume alone. Reported LOS patterns vary materially across acuity, disposition, and age cohorts, while longitudinal evidence indicates increasing historical demand. The strongest strategic application of these findings is to support case-mix-aware planning, targeted investigation of high-LOS patterns, and repeatable evidence monitoring as new aggregate data becomes available.
